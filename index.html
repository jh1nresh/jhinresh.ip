Savvy · personal place memory

The places worth keeping.

Food posts and travel links are easy to save and hard to find again.

I’m designing Savvy around three steps: save the source, review the place match, and keep the confirmed place as a Map Stamp.

iOS beta on TestFlight. The public food map and the iOS app are separate surfaces.

Synthetic places. An illustrated workflow, not the app interface.

OUTPUT · print-shop collaboration

A job needs another shop.

One shop can have more work than it can make. Another can have room on its machines. OUTPUT is my prototype for connecting the two.

I’m prototyping the hand-off around job terms, a production partner, evidence, and explicit approval before payment release.

Devnet sandbox. The website does not offer production printing or production escrow.

A job between two shops. Pilot on a test network. No production printing. Synthetic shapes; not a promise of jobs.

EZNest · artwork nesting

Make room for the parts.

Before a design reaches a cutting machine, the artwork has to fit the material.

I’m exploring how to arrange parts, add cut lines, and hand a file to a Hot folder.

Workflow preview. Synthetic shapes, not a measurement. Final settings and RIP compatibility are still being confirmed.

Loose parts and a material roll. Illustration with synthetic shapes. Not a measurement; a nested layout is not always better than a hand layout.

Maiat + Dojo · agent trust

Before an agent takes action.

An agent can propose an action. The quote, consent, and final result still need to be clear.

In Maiat and Dojo, I’m connecting agent reputation with explicit approval and receipts people can inspect.

Public protocol and application artifacts. Individual deployments have their own scope.

Quote, approval, and a receipt. Illustrated approval workflow. No real approval, signature, or payment is performed.
