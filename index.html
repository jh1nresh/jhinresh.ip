Chapter two / Places

The places
worth keeping.

Food posts and travel links are easy to save and hard to find again. Savvy is where I’m working on that problem.

Save a link, check the place match, and turn a confirmed place into a Map Stamp. Keep the source so you can remember why you saved it.

iOS beta on TestFlight. The public food map and the iOS app are separate surfaces.

Chapter three / Print shops

A job needs
another shop.

One shop can have more work than it can make. Another can have room on its machines. OUTPUT is my prototype for connecting those two situations.

The workflow brings together job terms, a production partner, evidence, and approval before payment release.

Devnet sandbox. The website does not offer production printing or production escrow.

Chapter four / Production

Make room
for the parts.

Before a design reaches a cutting machine, the artwork has to fit the material.

EZNest explores arranging parts, adding cut lines, and handing the prepared file to a Hot folder. The illustration introduces the parts, material roll, and nesting workstation behind that workflow.

Workflow preview. Synthetic shapes, not a measurement. Final settings and RIP compatibility are still being confirmed.
