The places
worth keeping.

Food posts and travel links are easy to save and hard to find again.

I’m designing Savvy around three steps: save the source, review the place match, and keep the confirmed place as a Map Stamp.

OUTPUTWORK IN PROGRESS

Chapter three / Print shops

A job needs
another shop.

One shop can have more work than it can make. Another can have room on its machines. OUTPUT is my prototype for connecting the two.

I’m prototyping the hand-off around job terms, a production partner, evidence, and explicit approval before payment release.

Devnet sandbox. The website does not offer production printing or production escrow.

Chapter four / Production

Make room
for the parts.

Before a design reaches a cutting machine, the artwork has to fit the material.

I’m exploring how to arrange parts, add cut lines, and hand a file to a Hot folder.

Workflow preview. Synthetic shapes, not a measurement. Final settings and RIP compatibility are still being confirmed.

takes action.
